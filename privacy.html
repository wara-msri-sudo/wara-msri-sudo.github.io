Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Pug Morning Brief ("the app") is a personal tool operated by its owner (github.com/wara-msri-sudo). It is used only to publish videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

What the app accesses

  • TikTok account information that TikTok provides when the owner signs in: the account's open ID and basic profile information (display name, avatar).
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, so the app can upload and publish videos that the owner has approved.

The app does not access any other TikTok user's data, does not read messages, and does not collect information from viewers of the published videos.

How it is used

The information above is used only to upload and publish the owner's approved videos to the owner's own TikTok account, and to check that a post succeeded.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner. Nothing is stored on a server operated by the app. The data is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app is in use. To remove the app's access at any time, revoke it in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). The owner then deletes the local token file.

Third parties

Videos and the data needed to publish them are sent only to TikTok, under TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: github.com/wara-msri-sudo.
